About

From the forecourt to the feed.

32, based in Warwickshire / Stratford-upon-Avon. If I'm not filming and editing, I'm in the gym, playing golf, or reading some sort of sci-fi fantasy book.

At home, my partner is well established in UGC too, having worked with 100+ brands, and together we've got a 6-year-old daughter who loves helping out on shoots.

I spent years in car sales, reading a room in the first ten seconds and knowing exactly what to say next. Turns out that's the same job as writing a hook that stops someone scrolling.

Now I write and shoot content for brands that need to sound human. No corporate voice, no filler, just what a real customer would actually say.

  • Social media management - I run the feeds for four different businesses, so I'm filming and editing most days, brief or not, which keeps me across every trend as it happens.
  • Gym access - I can film fitness and gym content on location whenever a brief calls for it.
  • Van hire - happy to hire one for the day for a tradie-style shoot.
  • On-location shoots - travelling to your site is never an issue.
  • Tidy home setup - a clean, well-lit space ready for aesthetic, lifestyle-style shots whenever a brief needs one.
